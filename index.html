About us

Halden is a family-run design-build firm based in Manhattan Beach. We build new custom homes and remodel existing ones, with one team taking your project from the first sketch to the final walkthrough.

Building near the beach comes with its own rules: tight lots, coastal permits, height limits and neighbors a few feet away. We've worked through all of it for nearly two decades, so your project stays on schedule and your street stays friendly.

How we work

One team, one point of contact and a fixed price before we break ground.

  1. Site visit

    We walk the property with you, talk through what you want and give you an honest early budget range.

  2. Design and permits

    Our designers draw the plans, and we handle engineering, coastal review and permits with your city.

  3. Fixed-price contract

    You get a detailed, line-by-line price and schedule. No surprise change orders for things we should have caught.

  4. Build

    A dedicated site lead runs the job and sends you a photo update every week.

  5. Walkthrough and warranty

    We finish with a walkthrough and punch list, and back the work with a two-year warranty.
